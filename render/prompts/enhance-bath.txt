WHY A MASK AND NOT A REGENERATION
The brief is to make the bathroom less plain "without changing the originality of
the image — the real one remains intact". A masked inpaint is the only way to
honour both halves of that: every pixel OUTSIDE the mask is the original
photograph, untouched, bit for bit. A reference-driven model (seedream_v4_5,
flux_kontext) would redraw the whole frame and the room would stop being this
room. Paint the mask over the upper wall, the ceiling and the lighting only.
Leave the basin, the mirror, the fittings, the door, the floor and the window
UNMASKED — those are what make it recognisably this bathroom.

PROMPT
The same bathroom, same layout, same fittings, same camera position, same
daylight from the same window. Warm the upper wall to a soft lime plaster with a
faint natural texture. Add a slim warm strip light above the mirror and a small
recessed niche in the tiled wall holding folded white towels and a plant. Keep
the existing basin, mirror, taps, door and floor exactly as they are. Photoreal,
natural colour, no stylisation. Empty and unoccupied, no people, no figures,
architectural, tasteful. No text, no captions, no logos.

AFTER
Compare against the original at full size. If any unmasked area has shifted, the
mask leaked — redo it tighter. The point of this step is a bathroom that still
photographs as the real one.
