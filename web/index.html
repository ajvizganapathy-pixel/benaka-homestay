Our story

A quiet home in the hills, made for slow stays.

Benaka sits among the coffee, rain and hills of Kodagu. Rooms open onto a long verandah and a courtyard that comes alive during the monsoon. Meals are cooked here and shared around the table, while the days unfold at their own pace.
